Who We Are

IK CONSTRUCTION COMPANY-UGANDA is a local founded construction company dealing in the development sector of commercial and residential infrastructure development in Uganda. Our main aim is to ensure the accessibility of commercial and residential infrastructure construction services to all citizens construction projects, government construction projects and investors construction projects in Uganda and along the globe. This company began it's operations in 2012 and by this time we have managed to serve an estimate of 180 to 450 satisfied clients with the commercial and residential infrastructure development services.
